Route 7's RapidRide upgrade, the R Line, goes to the King County Council for route and station approval in early 2027

October 29, 2026 · Rainier Valley Dispatch, issue 3 · All Rainier Valley news

The line runs about eight miles on South Jackson Street and Rainier Avenue South, from downtown to the Rainier Beach station, on a $91.2 million budget. Route 7 carries more than 11,000 riders a day. Metro is also working out how service continues south of South Henderson Street once the R Line ends at the station. King County Metro
