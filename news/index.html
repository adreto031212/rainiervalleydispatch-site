What changed in Rainier Valley.

Every story the Rainier Valley Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 12, 2026

The City Council's budget committee starts voting Friday, November 13, at 9:30 AM, and the morning opens with public comment

Budget Chair Dan Strauss's balancing package, released November 3, keeps the money for traffic safety projects on Rainier Avenue, in his office's words "critical investments." The committee spent…

Rainier Avenue South between South Fisher Place and Sturtevant Avenue South is in line for skid-resistant pavement

It is one of four stretches in a $9.7 million federal Safe Streets and Roads for All grant SDOT announced November 2.

Construction has started across from the Mount Baker station

On October 15 the coalition broke ground on the 3.8-acre former University of Washington laundry site.

when the power goes out

Report an outage to Seattle City Light at (206) 684-3000, with your account number or the phone number on your account ready.

November 5, 2026

The Seattle Public Library is closed on Wednesday, November 11, for Veterans Day, at every branch, including Columbia City, Beacon Hill and Rainier Beach

The library's holiday page lists the closure for all locations.

before the first freeze

Keep the thermostat at 55 degrees or higher, day and night, even when you are away.

October 29, 2026

Ballots go in a drop box by 8 PM Tuesday, November 3

King County Elections' drop-box map lists four in the valley: Beacon Hill Library, 2821 Beacon Avenue South; Rainier Community Center, 4600 38th Avenue South; NewHolly Neighborhood Campus, 7054 32nd…

South Henderson Street's two-way protected bike lane is open, from the Rainier Beach station toward Seward Park Avenue South

SDOT built it on the north side behind concrete curbs, with bus and bike platforms at Renton Avenue South and Rainier Avenue South, new crosswalks and a repaved stretch of the Chief Sealth Trail.

Route 7's RapidRide upgrade, the R Line, goes to the King County Council for route and station approval in early 2027

The line runs about eight miles on South Jackson Street and Rainier Avenue South, from downtown to the Rainier Beach station, on a $91.2 million budget.

The Beacon Hill station elevator outage is off Sound Transit's alert list

Elevator 2 had been out since October 14; on October 27 the service alerts listed no Beacon Hill, Mount Baker, Columbia City, Othello or Rainier Beach station alert.

Clocks fall back one hour at 2 AM Sunday, November 1

The 1 AM hour happens twice.

what goes in the food and yard cart

Meat, bones, shells, dairy, fish and moldy leftovers all go in the food and yard waste cart, with leaves, branches and plants.

October 22, 2026

The Beacon Avenue South survey closes October 31, and the online meeting is next Thursday, October 29, 6 to 7 PM

SDOT is in preliminary design for Beacon Avenue South from South Spokane Street to 39th Avenue South, including bike connections to the protected lanes on South Columbian Way and South Myrtle Street.

Jefferson, Rainier and Van Asselt community centers are in line to replace their fossil-fuel equipment and go fully electric

Seattle Parks' package, about $13 million, also covers the Genesee maintenance headquarters and a new roof at Van Asselt.

The Columbia City Farmers Market closes its season next Wednesday, October 28

It runs 3 to 7 PM at 37th Avenue South and South Edmunds Street, one block from the light rail station.

Elevator 2 at Beacon Hill station is still out, a week after the October 14 outage

Sound Transit lists it as unavailable until further notice; the station's other elevators are in service.

Kubota Garden's maple tree sale is this Saturday, October 24, 9 AM to 1 PM

Pickup is drive-through at 9817 55th Avenue South.

clear the drain, not the street

Leaves on a storm grate back water up onto the street and toward basements once the November rain starts.

October 17, 2026

SDOT has started designing Beacon Avenue South from South Spokane Street to 39th Avenue South, and the first chance to weigh in is today

The plan covers repaving and street changes for people walking, biking, driving and riding the bus, and extends bike lanes from South Spokane Street to the protected lanes on South Columbian Way and…

One elevator at Beacon Hill station has been out since Wednesday, with no return date

Sound Transit lists elevator 2 as unavailable after an unscheduled outage that began at 5:24 AM on October 14; riders use the station's other elevators.

The Columbia City Farmers Market has two Wednesdays left: October 21 and October 28

It runs 3 to 7 PM at 37th Avenue South and South Edmunds Street, just off Rainier Avenue South, and closes the season on the 28th.

Kubota Garden's free fall color tours start at 10 AM today

Six guided walks, October 17, 18, 24, 25, 31 and November 1, each about two hours over 1.5 miles of gravel paths; registration is required and each tour takes 40 people.

November 2, not October 31

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2.
