November 2, not October 31

October 17, 2026 · Rainier Valley Dispatch, issue 1 · All Rainier Valley news

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2. The usual date is October 31; when it falls on a weekend, the deadline moves to the next business day. Mail a check postmarked by the due date to King County Treasury, 201 S. Jackson St., Suite 710, Seattle, WA 98104, with your account number on it, or pay online (a service fee applies). Questions: 206-263-2890. King County Treasury
