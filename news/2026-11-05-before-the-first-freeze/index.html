Rainier Valley · One useful thing

before the first freeze

November 5, 2026 · Rainier Valley Dispatch, issue 4 · All Rainier Valley news

Keep the thermostat at 55 degrees or higher, day and night, even when you are away. Open the cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls so indoor heat reaches those pipes, and shut off and insulate outdoor faucets. If a pipe bursts, close the main shut-off valve at once, and know where that valve is before the cold arrives. Never thaw a frozen pipe with an open flame. Seattle Public Utilities winter tips
