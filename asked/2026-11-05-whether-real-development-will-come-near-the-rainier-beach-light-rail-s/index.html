Whether real development will come near the Rainier Beach light rail station

November 5, 2026 · Rainier Valley Dispatch, issue 4 · Every answered question

A reader in Rainier Beach asked whether real development will come near the Rainier Beach light rail station. On June 2, 2026, the Seattle City Council voted 9 to 0 to pass Council Bill 121196, which caps structures on a lot on South Trenton Street, between 42nd Avenue South and Martin Luther King Jr. Way South, at 125 feet, a site less than a quarter mile from the station. The Tubman Center for Health and Freedom owns most of the lot and plans a 26,000-square-foot health center, due to be completed in winter 2027 to 2028 at an estimated $42 million. Mount Baker Housing, which owns the rest, says its affordable housing project there is at least a decade away. South Seattle Emerald Know a neighbor asking the same thing?
